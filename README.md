# 📊 Interactive Excel Dashboard Portfolio

Dokumentasi portofolio dashboard interaktif berbasis **Microsoft Excel** yang mencakup 3 studi kasus analitika bisnis: penjualan ritel supermarket, pemantauan penjualan otomotif, dan analitika risiko retensi karyawan (HR Analytics). Seluruh visualisasi, analisis data, dan arsitektur dashboard dirangkum dalam dokumen PDF terlampir.

## 📑 Ringkasan Studi Kasus dalam Dokumen

### 1. Case Study 1: Supermarket Sales Dashboard
* **Sumber Dataset:** [Kaggle - Supermarket Sales Cleaned Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/muhdaniyal/supermarket-sales-cleaned-dataset)
* **Fokus Analisis:** Evaluasi performa transaksi di 3 cabang kota utama (Yangon, Naypyitaw, Mandalay), tren penjualan bulanan, kontribusi lini produk, marjin kotor, dan skor kepuasan pelanggan (*customer rating*).
* **Ringkasan Metrik (KPIs):**
  * **Total Transactions:** 1,000
  * **Total Revenue:** $338,346.12
  * **Total COGS:** $307,587.38
  * **Gross Income:** $15,379.37
  * **Total Quantity Sold:** 5,510 unit
  * **Average Rating:** 6.97 / 10
* **Fitur Utama:** *Monthly Sales Trend*, *Revenue by City/Branch*, *Revenue by Product Line*, *Rating by Product*, serta filter interaktif (*Date Timeline, City, Customer Type, Product Line*).

---

### 2. Case Study 2: Car Sales Performance Dashboard
* **Sumber Dataset:** [Kaggle - Car Sales Report](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/missionjee/car-sales-report)
* **Fokus Analisis:** Analisis penjualan mobil komprehensif untuk memantau performa jaringan dealer, kontribusi regional, preferensi model/merek, tipe bodi kendaraan, dan jenis transmisi/mesin.
* **Ringkasan Metrik (KPIs):**
  * **Total Transactions:** 10,393 unit
  * **Total Revenue:** $294,029.5K
  * **Average Revenue per Car:** $28.3K
  * **Average Customer Annual Income:** $832.6K
* **Fitur Utama:** Tampilan visual multi-sudut pandang (*Company Performance, Regional Analysis, Dealer Ranking, Types of Cars Breakdown*) yang didukung filter dinamis tahun, kuartal, bulan, wilayah dealer, dan *Car Model Slicer*.

### 3. Case Study 3: Employee Retention & Attrition Risk Analytics
* **Sumber Dataset:** [Kaggle - HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Risk Levels](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/bertnardomariouskono/hr-analytics-employee-attrition-and-risk-levels)
* **Fokus Analisis:** Pemetaan profil risiko atrisi/turnover karyawan (*Low Risk, Medium Risk, High Risk*) berdasarkan kepuasan kerja, keseimbangan hidup-kerja (*work-life balance*), jam kerja bulanan, masa kerja, dan latar belakang pendidikan.
* **Ringkasan Metrik (KPIs):**
  * **Total Employees:** 15,000 karyawan (7,507 Perempuan | 7,493 Laki-laki)
  * **Distribusi Risiko:** 
    * Low Risk (0): 50.00% (7,500 karyawan)
    * Medium Risk (1): 35.00% (5,250 karyawan)
    * High Risk (2): 15.00% (2,250 karyawan)
  * **Average Monthly Hours:** 170.44 jam/bulan
  * **Job Satisfaction Score:** 2.91 / 4.00
  * **Work-Life Balance Score:** 2.70 / 4.00
* **Fitur Utama:** Analisis korelasi risiko berdasarkan divisi/peran kerja, tingkat pendidikan formal, kelompok masa kerja (*years at company*), dan tingkat kepuasan kerja.
